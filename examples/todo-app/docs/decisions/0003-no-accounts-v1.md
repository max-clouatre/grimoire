# 0003 · No accounts in v1

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-09-02

## Context
Accounts bring auth, password resets, privacy policy and hosting cost, with none of it serving the "one fast list" goal.

## Decision
v1 is single-user and self-hosted. Export (M3) is how people move their data.

## Consequences
- Much less to build and secure.
- Sharing and multi-device sync are out until there is clear demand.
